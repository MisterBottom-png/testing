# Asset attribution

Every non-code file shipped in A-Studio, with author, source and licence. Add the row in the same
commit as the file. `cargo xtask assets` (ported in P0) fails on any file under `assets/` without a row.
The upstream rows to carry over, and the ones to drop, are in `docs/data/assets.csv`.

| Path | Title | Author | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/app-icon/a-studio.svg` | A-Studio app icon master (placeholder) | A-Studio contributors | Original; generated by `scripts/icon-svgs.py`, PNG exported with `scripts/render-svg.js` | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
| `assets/app-icon/a-studio-1024.png` | A-Studio app icon, 1024 px PNG (placeholder) | A-Studio contributors | Original; generated by `scripts/icon-svgs.py`, PNG exported with `scripts/render-svg.js` | MIT OR Apache-2.0 |
