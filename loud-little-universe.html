How it works

  • A beam of light spins around the sun.
  • When the beam hits a planet, the planet makes a sound.
  • Tap a ring to drop a planet. Tap a planet to take it away.
  • Each ring is one sound. The two outer rings play notes.
  • Tap the sun to stop and start.
  • Drag in the dark to push the stars around.
  • No sound? Check that your phone is not on silent.

Made from scratch for MisterBottom, who builds drum kits. No pictures, no sound files, no borrowed code. Every star and every sound is drawn by the page as you watch.
