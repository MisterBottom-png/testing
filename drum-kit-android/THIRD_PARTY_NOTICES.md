- Creator and recording engineer: Jes Eiler

- Source archive: `https://drumgizmo.org/kits/Aasimonster/aasimonster2_1.zip`

- License text: `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`
- Project information: `https://www.drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:the_aasimonster`

Used for centre, off-centre, edge-expression, and rimshot snare samples. The application preprocesses selected source recordings into a stereo runtime bank by mixing channels, trimming, aligning, fading, globally normalizing, and quantizing to 48 kHz interleaved PCM. The edge-expression samples are processed derivatives of the off-centre recordings.

Used for cross-stick round robins. The source site describes its samples as freeware and royalty-free and explicitly permits embedding samples in applications, games, and virtual instruments. The source WAVs are resampled from 44.1 kHz to 48 kHz, trimmed, aligned, globally normalized with the rest of the bank, and quantized to interleaved PCM.
